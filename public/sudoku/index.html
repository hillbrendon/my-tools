How to play

Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the numbers 1 to 9, with no repeats.

  • Tap a square, then tap a number to fill it in. Tap the same number again to remove it.
  • Notes lets you pencil in small possible numbers. They clear themselves when you fill in a matching number nearby.
  • Hint fills in the selected square (or a random one) for you.
  • The daily puzzle is the same for everyone. It's easiest on Monday and hardest on Sunday.
  • On a keyboard: arrow keys to move, 1–9 to fill, Backspace to erase, N for notes, Ctrl+Z to undo.

Your game saves automatically on this device.
