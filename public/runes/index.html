How to play

Find the hidden five-letter word in six tries.

To start, type any real five-letter word (for example STARE or CRANE) and press Enter. There are no clues before your first guess. After each guess the rune stones change colour to show how close you are:

Grey: the letter isn't in the word.

Use the clues for your next guess: keep ember letters in place, move blue letters to a new spot, and avoid grey ones. The keyboard is coloured the same way.

  • Daily is the same word for everyone, with a new one at midnight. Share your result without spoiling the answer.
  • Practice gives you unlimited random words.
  • Guesses must be real words. Letters can repeat.
