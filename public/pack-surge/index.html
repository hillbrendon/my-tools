Storm the fortress!

  • Drag left and right to aim your wolf launcher. It fires on its own.
  • Send wolves through ember gates to multiply them. Avoid red gates.
  • Wolves and draugr destroy each other. Reach the fortress to smash its walls.

  • Don't let the draugr reach your den!
