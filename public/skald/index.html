How to play

  • Swipe across the letters in the circle to spell a word, then lift your finger. Or tap letters one at a time and press Enter.
  • Find every word shown in the boxes to finish the level. Words must be 3 or more letters.
  • Found a real word that isn't in the boxes? It's a bonus word, worth ᚠ2.
  • Earn coins (ᚠ) by finishing levels. Spend 20 on a hint to reveal a letter.
  • Shuffle rearranges the letters, which can help you spot new words.
  • There are 400 levels. They get longer and harder as you go, and your progress saves on this device.
